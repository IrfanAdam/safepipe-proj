The problem

Running a pipeline means thousands of moving parts — and no single view of them.

01

Scattered, unverified records

Maintenance, inspections, certifications, and maps sit in separate systems. No one can say with confidence who did what, where, and when.

02

Surprise audit gaps

Gaps surface when the auditor asks, not before. Preparing takes weeks of reconciling and chasing paper.

03

Crews dispatched blind

At assignment time, no one sees whose qualification is about to lapse or which asset is most at risk. Small issues turn into expensive ones.

The idea · Why choose SafePipe

A digital twin that understands your infrastructure — not just stores data about it.

Digital twin

Every segment, checkpoint, and piece of equipment has a live virtual counterpart carrying its full history: inspections, repairs, mapped high-consequence areas, test results. The network you operate is the network you see.

Ontology of real-world data

SafePipe models how things relate: this segment has these checkpoints, this work order applies to that asset, this task requires an operator with a current qualification. Explicit relationships turn separate silos into one system you can question.

How it works · Services

Observe. Decide. Act.

Observe

Capture at the source

Crews record patrols, inspections, and quality checks against the asset they are standing at. An 83-question form system covers patrol, MOC, QC, evaluation scoring, and mileage.

Every record lands on the right asset.
Decide

Surface what needs attention

The workforce directory shows each person's workload and OQ certifications with expiry dates. Expiring certs and overdue assets come to you before they become findings.

Qualification visible at assignment.
Act

Send the right crew to the right job

Each work order carries the map, client, assignee, and equipment checklist on one screen. Crews go to the highest-priority work — and only qualified operators are assigned.
