Components · Data · Progress

The fill never speaks without its count.

Progress is the 4px pill bar that reports completion for tasks, qualifications, and work orders — living inside cards, rows, and page headers, never as its own surface. Use it when work is partially done; reach for status when a record needs a state flag with no percent behind it.

  • 01

    Every bar ships its counts row. The card form's "22 out of 33" pair — value in color.text.primary, remainder in color.semantic.warning at text.sm fontweight.medium — is the human-readable equivalent of aria-valuenow. The bar alone never carries the value.

    Reject: a bare 4px strip with the percent only in a tooltip — at a glance it is decoration.

  • 02

    An inner strip, never a surface. Track is color.surface.input at radius.md, fill color.accent.violet (or color.brand.sky), knob radius.full shown only for scrub. card.md owns the container, list-row.md owns the row.

    Reject: a progress card wrapped around a single bar — one bar per record, housed, not showcased.

  • 03

    Incomplete is not failed. Pending stays color.semantic.warning; color.semantic.danger is reserved for overdue or errored records. Hold the last known percent while loading — never animate from zero when the number is already known.

    Reject: a bar that replays 0→45% on every render — motion where a number belongs.
