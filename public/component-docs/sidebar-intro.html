Components · Navigation · Sidebar

The rail never scrolls, never sells, never spins.

Sidebar is the persistent minimized icon rail that keeps the seven primary sections one click away while content scrolls. It navigates between sections; reach for the header when the page needs a title, actions, or a back stack.

  • 01

    Navigation only — never actions. The rail holds the logo slot, client avatar, and tab.md nav items split into primary and utility groups by a color.border.default divider. Commit buttons belong in the header toolbar or content, on color.surface.card over elevation.1.

    Reject: a "New work order" button parked in the rail — the moment the rail sells, wayfinding dies.

  • 02

    Minimized is the only collapsed form. All 28 live instances are icon-only; items reuse tab.md exactly at 60×44 with spacing.xs gaps. An expanded label-bearing drawer must be specced before it ships — never improvised.

    Reject: a forked nav-item style with its own hover fill — second styles drift, then multiply.

  • 03

    One active section, ring on the item. A single aria-current="page" carries the tab.md selected state; focus rings color.brand.sky inside the item, never on the rail. Section switches show progress.md in content — the rail itself never spins.

    Reject: two highlights in the rail, or labels at color.text.muted on the active item.
