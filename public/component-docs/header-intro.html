Components · Navigation · Header

One app bar per screen. Titles live inside content.

Header is the persistent top band — identity, date, search, page actions — plus the title blocks that introduce each screen. Use the app bar once at the window edge; reach for the section form inside content, the toolbar form above lists.

  • 01

    Bar — never owns the commit. The app bar holds brand, date, alerts and avatar on var(--sp-surface-card); commit primaries live in content. The toolbar form is search plus icon buttons at var(--sp-spacing-md) gaps.

    Reject: a navy Save floating in the app bar next to the avatar.

  • 02

    Title — one xl per page. The title block carries a single var(--sp-font-display) var(--sp-text-xl) semibold heading over its overline and meta, closed by a var(--sp-border-default) divider; a second heading that size means the page has two owners.

    Reject: three competing headings stacked above one table.

  • 03

    Chrome — never steals focus. The bar itself is not focusable; the var(--sp-sky) ring lands on the focused icon or button only, and sticky bars offset scroll so focused content is never covered.

    Reject: a sticky bar swallowing the focused field it just scrolled past.
