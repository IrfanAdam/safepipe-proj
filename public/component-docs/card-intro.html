Components · Data · Card

One record per card. Never a second surface inside.

Card is the elevated surface that groups one record — inspection, work order, report — with its status, tags, and metadata, so list pages scan as a stack of equal-weight objects. Use it when a record needs depth; reach for list-row when records must be compared across a shared row.

  • 01

    Anatomy — surface · title · tags · strip · footer. color.surface.card at radius.lg with spacing.lg / spacing.md padding; title text.lg fontweight.semibold; tag.md row at spacing.xs; optional progress.md strip; divider plus attribution footer.

    Reject: a card nested inside a card — chips and strips compose, surfaces never do.

  • 02

    Elevation rests at 2, lifts to 3. Resting shadow is elevation.2; hover alone earns elevation.3 with a color.border.strong border. Selected is a color.brand.navy border, never a shadow bump.

    Reject: two resting shadows stacked for emphasis — doubled elevation reads as a bug.

  • 03

    Titles stay lg; pages own xl. Card titles never exceed text.lg — page titles belong to header.md at text.xl. Errors report inline in color.semantic.danger; loading swaps the body for color.surface.input skeleton blocks.

    Reject: a 24px card title competing with the page header — hierarchy collapses.
