Spec — a11y + do / don't

The dot is aria-hidden; the label carries meaning and goes live with aria-live when it changes. Dots need 3:1 against the card at their size — grow to var(--sp-spacing-sm) if the token fails. The trailing control is its own tab stop with its own name.

  • 01

    Do: ship every colored dot with its text label — danger alone never means non-compliant.

  • 02

    Do: use one card form per list — horizontal by default, vertical for rails.

  • 03

    Don't: recolor a state to fit a palette, or give the card dialog radius and padding.
