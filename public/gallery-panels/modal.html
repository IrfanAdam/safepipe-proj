Spec — a11y + contract

Do: right-aligned footer with cancel left of confirm, one navy primary, measured padding pair. Don't: open a second dialog, or use a dialog for long-form reading.

Spec · a11y + related

A11y — role="dialog" aria-modal="true", labelled by the title, described by the subtitle; focus trapped and restored; background inert; Esc closes; title first heading, unclipped at 200% zoom. Related — field owns the grid · button owns the footer pair · card owns long-form reading.
