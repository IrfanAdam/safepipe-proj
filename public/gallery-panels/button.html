Spec — a11y + do / don't

Native button with a 44px minimum target; inverse on navy clears contrast at var(--sp-text-md); loading keeps its accessible name under aria-busy and the ring is never clipped by dialog overflow.

  • 01

    Do: ship one navy commit per region — a second primary is a decision, not a style.

  • 02

    Do: name every icon-only square; the target is not a label.

  • 03

    Don't: spend danger on anything reversible, or mix a third padding pair into the set.
