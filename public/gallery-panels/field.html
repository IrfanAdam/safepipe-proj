Spec — a11y + do / don't

Every input carries a programmatic label; errors wire aria-describedby with aria-invalid and keep focus after a failed submit. Touch height pads toward 44px and placeholder contrast must clear 4.5:1.

  • 01

    Do: keep the label visible at every state — placeholders hint, they never name.

  • 02

    Do: change border and message together; never signal error with color alone.

  • 03

    Don't: invent a third height, copy the legacy pill field, or move the label on error.
