Spec — states

Only the controls have states — the bar itself never focuses, hovers, or disables. Icon buttons take the input ground on hover, ring sky on focus, mute with aria-disabled when unavailable; loading swaps the meta line to muted placeholder text at the same metric.

Spec — a11y + do / don't

App bar is header with role="banner", once per page; section titles are real headings inside main. Icon-only controls need names and a var(--sp-spacing-sm) minimum target; sticky bars offset scroll for :target and focus.

  • 01

    Do: keep the section form inside content — only the app bar touches the window edge.

  • 02

    Do: give the title block at most one var(--sp-text-xl) heading per page.

  • 03

    Don't: park commit actions in the bar, or draw the divider in full black.
